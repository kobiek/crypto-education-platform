# Crypto Education Platform
## "Crypto Made Simple" - Learn Blockchain from Zero to Hero

A comprehensive, beginner-friendly crypto education platform designed to make blockchain technology accessible to everyone, including 12-year-olds!

## 🎯 Mission
To demystify cryptocurrency and blockchain technology through simple explanations, visual illustrations, and interactive learning experiences.

## 📚 Curriculum Overview

### **Level 1: Blockchain Basics** (Free)
- What is a blockchain? (with LEGO analogies)
- How does cryptocurrency work? (like digital money)
- Wallets and addresses (your digital piggy bank)
- Mining and consensus (how the network stays secure)

### **Level 2: Getting Started** ($97)
- How to buy your first crypto
- Setting up a wallet safely
- Understanding exchanges
- Security best practices

### **Level 3: Trading Fundamentals** ($197)
- Reading price charts (candlesticks explained)
- Basic trading strategies
- Risk management
- Psychology of trading

### **Level 4: DeFi Deep Dive** ($297)
- What is DeFi? (decentralized finance)
- Yield farming and staking
- Liquidity pools
- Smart contracts explained

### **Level 5: Advanced Strategies** ($497)
- Portfolio management
- Advanced trading techniques
- NFT strategies
- Building in Web3
